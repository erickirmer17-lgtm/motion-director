# THE AI EDITING ERA — Claude spec ad (direction v1)

**Format:** 1080×1920 · 60 fps · 15.0 s (900 f) · ends on the Claude logo
**Line:** *The AI editing era has begun…*
**Feel:** historic → futuristic · clean · fast · light mode

## Idea
Every transition is an edit. Claude (the terracotta spark, used as the cursor) edits history.
The material is public-domain history of the moving image and of machines that write and draw:
Muybridge, Marey, phenakistoscopes, Moviola, camera obscura, the Turk, Jaquet-Droz automata,
Diderot plates, Dürer hands, marble heads, Metropolis. Futuristic counterparts come from GPT-Image-2:
robot hands in the same carbon/gold design as the lime robot-hands piece, a marble android bust,
and an Encyclopédie-style engraving of a robot arm splicing film.
Lime UI (selection boxes, playhead, razor, highlights) is painted over the B&W images.

## Palette / type
- White `#FFFFFF` (no beige), ink `#0E0E0E`, **lime `#C6F432`** for the edit layer, **Claude terracotta `#D97757`** for the spark only.
- Images: strict B&W. Engravings are ink-extracted (paper → transparent) so they layer cleanly.
- Type: **Inter Display** (future) × **Instrument Serif** (history). Words get "edited" from serif into sans.
- No eyebrow labels, no UI chrome text, no timecodes.

|---|---|---|
| 0–48 | **cut**: serif word, a lime razor slices it, the halves slide apart | CUT |

| 236–300 | a phenakistoscope disc spins (the figures actually animate); the spark scrubs it like a jog wheel | SCRUB |
| 300–400 | Creation of Adam, vertical: a Dürer/Michelangelo hand from below, the robot hand from above; the Claude spark ignites between the fingertips; hand montage match-cut on the spark | MATCH CUT |
| 400–540 | clean 3D: a flat timeline tilts into 3D; clips are glossy slabs carrying the engravings; a lime glass playhead razors through; the camera dives into a clip | ASSEMBLE |
| 540–620 | accelerating montage → grid of history that auto-sorts → camera obscura | SORT |
| 600–700 | **camera**: select "cam", razor, ripple-delete → **era**; the line builds | RIPPLE DELETE |

| 770–900 | the three dots merge into a dot, the dot blooms into the spark, the wordmark wipes in, hold | RENDER |

## Sound
112.5 BPM grid (32 f/beat). A soft sub pulse on the downbeats; per-cut micro-ticks; film-gate flutter
under the historic section; razor "shhk" on every cut; dry typewriter clicks; airy whooshes on moves;
a riser into the line; half a beat of near-silence; a warm chord + shimmer on the spark → logo.
